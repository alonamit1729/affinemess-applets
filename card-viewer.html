Card Viewer

One card at a time from the catalog of n points with d distances: planar sets of n points whose pairwise distances take only d different values.

  • Every segment is coloured by its length; the panel lists the lengths, with the shortest as 1, and how many segments have each.
  • Hover a length in the panel, or a segment in the picture, to bring out every segment of that length. Click to keep it highlighted.
  • Hover a point to see its own segments, in their colours; the counts in the panel then count only the segments at that point. Click a point to keep it.
  • Shortest distances draws the shortest length in ink and the others as faint hairlines, which shows the skeleton of a big set. Point letters names the points.
  • Step through a cell with ‹ › or the arrow keys, pick another cell from the list, or click a card in the strip. The link button next to the card number copies a link to this card.

The viewer shows the catalog's rigid sets, the ones that cannot be deformed without changing which distances are equal. Families of sets that can flex live in the catalog itself. The coordinates are scaled so that the shortest distance is 1, and turned so that one shortest segment is horizontal.

To try building a set with straightedge and compass, open Distance Hunt.
