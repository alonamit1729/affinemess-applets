Set up

  1. Lay the baseplates edge to edge, 3 across and 2 deep (144 × 96 studs). The map's column and row numbers count studs from the far-left corner as seen from the front.
  2. Keep the model on the baseplates until it's finished. The second pillar doesn't join the strip until step 52, so the baseplates are what keep it lined up.

Reading a step

  • Each step builds one layer. In the picture, new bricks have bold outlines and full colour; earlier layers are faded.
  • Some steps continue with short parts (12·2, 12·3…). Each part works on one layer only. On the map, a yellow outline means place on top; a blue outline means press onto the underside of the bricks above. Parts with blue outlines also show a view from underneath.
  • Each step builds one layer. New bricks are coloured: yellow bricks go on top of what's there; blue bricks are pressed onto the underside of the bricks above them. Bricks in their own colours are already in place.
  • Some steps continue with short parts (12·2, 12·3…). Each part works on one layer only and does one kind of placement. Parts with blue bricks also show a view from underneath.
  • Faint outlines on the map show the neighbouring layer, for lining things up. Tap any brick to see its exact columns and rows.
