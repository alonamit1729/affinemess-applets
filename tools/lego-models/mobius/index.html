Brick Möbius Strip

3,557 bricks (2×4 and 2×2) · 85 layers

Before you start

The model goes up one layer per step, from the floor. Each page shows the model after the step and a top-down map of the one layer you're working on. Pieces that would hang out in the air while you build, and the two pillars, are built separately and then put in place.

Set up

  1. Lay the baseplates edge to edge, 3 across and 2 deep (144 × 96 studs). The map's column and row numbers count studs from the far-left corner as seen from the front, and the darker lines on the map, every 48 studs, are the seams between baseplates.
  2. Keep the model on the baseplates until it's finished; they keep everything lined up.

Reading a step

  • Each step builds one layer. In the picture, new bricks have bold outlines and full colour; earlier layers are faded.
  • Each step builds one layer. New bricks are yellow: they go on top of what's there. Bricks in their own colours are already in place.
  • Sub-builds. Some pieces stick out over empty space as the model goes up. Rather than building them in the air, you build each one on its own on the table (its pages say "sub-build"), then lower it onto the model in the step that uses it. On the map, a purple marks a sub-build being lowered into place, in the picture and on the map.
  • Pillars. Each grey pillar is a stack of crossed pairs of bricks, built on its own and stood on the baseplates (on the black ring) when the strip reaches it.
  • Faint outlines on the map show the neighbouring layer, for lining things up. Tap any brick to see its exact columns and rows.
