Looking around

Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move, double-click to switch between the whole picture and full size. At full size every stud is visible.

How it was made

Every brick is a real brick of the buildable model: the same brick list drives the 3D view and the instructions. Rendered in Blender (Cycles) from that list, with rounded stud edges and a glossy ABS-like material.
