Brick Trefoil Seifert Surface

8,379 bricks (2×4 and 2×2) · 84 layers

Before you start

A Seifert surface for the trefoil knot: one page of the Milnor fibration of z² + w³, a punctured torus whose single edge is the trefoil. It has two sides, so they get two colours: orange and azure. The white bricks trace the knot.

The model goes up one layer per step, from the floor. Each page shows the model after the step and a top-down map of the one layer you're working on.

Set up

  1. Lay the baseplates edge to edge, 2 across and 3 deep (96 × 144 studs). The map's column and row numbers count studs from the far-left corner as seen from the front.
  2. Keep the model on the baseplates until it's finished. It stands in separate pieces (the surface and three grey pillars) until step 27, so the baseplates keep everything lined up.
  3. It's heavy, about 19 kg. Once finished, move it on its baseplates, lifting from underneath.

Reading a step

  • Each step builds one layer. In the picture, new bricks have bold outlines and full colour; earlier layers are faded.
  • Some steps continue with short parts (12·2, 12·3…). Each part works on one layer only. On the map, a yellow outline means place on top; a blue outline means press onto the underside of the bricks above. Parts with blue outlines also show a view from underneath.
  • Faint outlines on the map show the neighbouring layer, for lining things up. Tap any brick to see its exact columns and rows.
