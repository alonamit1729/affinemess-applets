Lego models

Surfaces from topology, rebuilt as real brick models: standard 2×4 bricks, with 2×2s where a layer needs them, all studs up. Each model is a list of bricks that can actually be built, and everything here is drawn from that one list.

What you can open

Render: a photo-real picture you can zoom into down to the studs. 3D view: turn the model around, or slide through it layer by layer. Instructions: step-by-step pages with a picture and a top-down map of each layer.

Making them buildable

The surface is thickened to a band about four studs thick, cut into the Lego grid layer by layer, and each layer is tiled with bricks so that bricks overlap the joints below them. A computer check confirms that every brick, at the moment it is placed, clips onto a brick already there (pressed on top, or occasionally onto the underside of the brick above), that the whole model is one connected piece, and that it stands without tipping: grey pillars hold it up where needed.

The two surfaces

The Möbius strip is tilted 65° so that no part of the band is flatter than about 51°, which lets it go up like a curved wall. The trefoil surface is one page of the Milnor fibration of z² + w³, projected to keep its three-fold symmetry. It is a genus-one Seifert surface; its two sides are orange and azure, and the white bricks trace the knot.
