About

I'm Alon Amit — a mathematician by training (PhD in combinatorics from Hebrew University, under Nati Linial) who has spent his working life in and around financial technology, currently as a Managing Director at JPMorgan Chase.

On the side I keep a small arcade of mathematical sketches: competition problems I couldn't quite let go, applets I built while preparing for math circles, Desmos graphs that got out of hand, and the occasional paper. This site is where I'm starting to collect them.

If something on here is interesting, broken, or wrong, I'd love to hear about it.
