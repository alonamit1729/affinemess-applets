About

This is a growing collection of mathematical musings, doodles, writings and talks.

My math bio includes being a board member of Proof School since its inception, a frequent writer of mathematical answers on Quora, a regular leader of Math Circles in the Bay Area, and various appearances and talks. I have a Ph.D. in math from the Hebrew University, Jerusalem, and my Erdős number is 2.

Outside of math I work in tech and finance, jog, bike, play the piano and drink too much coconut juice.
